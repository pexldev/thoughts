# YouTube Thumbnail Brief — I could be wrong

---

## Brand Constants
- **Background:** Acid yellow — #D4FF00
- **Text color:** Near-black — #1A1A1A
- **Font:** Bold, condensed, slightly rough/textured — never clean or corporate
- **Mascot:** Bearded Indian guy, round glasses with one cracked lens, messy hair, grey t-shirt, flat illustration style, bold outlines
- **Logo mark:** Small cracked oval mirror — always bottom right corner

---

## Thumbnail Layout

```
[Acid yellow background]

[3-5 word episode hook — large, bold, left or center]
[Mascot — right side, pose matching episode mood]
[Cracked mirror logo — bottom right, small]
```

---

## Text Rules
- 3 to 5 words MAX on the thumbnail — not the full title, just the punch
- All caps, bold, slightly grungy texture on the font
- Examples:
  - Omelette episode → **BROKEN. EVERY TIME.**
  - Comparison episode → **HE'S TWO LEVELS AHEAD.**
  - Corporate episode → **BHAGWAN AUR CORPORATE**
  - Cars/lifestyle episode → **YOUR SUV. MY I10. SAME TRAFFIC.**

---

## Mascot Pose by Episode Mood

| Episode mood | Pose |
|---|---|
| Heavy / introspective | Sitting, chin on hand, looking down |
| Funny / observational | Standing, hands in pockets, sideways glance |
| Everyday / relatable | At desk with mic and cracked mug |
| Confused / lost | Walking, not paying attention |
| Frustrated | Arms slightly out, "what is happening" energy |

```
Pose: [POSE FROM TABLE ABOVE]. Flat illustration style, bold outlines, 
no complex shading. Acid yellow background shows through.

Bottom right corner: Small minimal cracked oval mirror frame logo mark in black.

No gradients. No drop shadows. No extra elements. Raw and unpolished feeling.
```

---

## What To Never Do
- More than 5 words of text on the thumbnail
- Stock photos or real photos
- Gradients, glows, drop shadows
- Busy or cluttered layout
- Anything that looks like a motivational content page
- Pure white or pure black background
