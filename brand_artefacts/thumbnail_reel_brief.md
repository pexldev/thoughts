# Thumbnail & Reel Image Brief — Side Effects of Thinking

---

## Brand Constants (Never Change)
- **Background color:** Acid yellow — #D4FF00
- **Text color:** Near-black — #1A1A1A
- **Font style:** Bold, condensed, slightly rough/textured
- **Mascot:** Bearded Indian guy, round glasses with one cracked lens, messy hair, grey t-shirt, flat illustration style
- **Tagline** (optional use): think. overthink. repeat.

---

## YouTube Thumbnail

### Format
- 1280x720px
- Horizontal / landscape

### Layout
```
[Acid yellow background]

[Episode topic in large bold text — left or center]
[Mascot — right side, bust or full body depending on pose]
[Small cracked mirror logo — bottom right corner]
```

### Text rules
- Episode title on thumbnail should be SHORT — 3 to 5 words max
- Not the full episode title — just the punch word or phrase
- Examples:
  - Episode about omelette → "BROKEN. EVERY TIME."
  - Episode about comparison → "HE'S TWO LEVELS AHEAD."
  - Episode about corporate → "BHAGWAN AUR CORPORATE"

### Mascot pose per episode mood
| Episode mood | Mascot pose to use |
|---|---|
| Introspective / heavy | Sitting, chin on hand, looking down |
| Funny / observational | Standing, hands in pockets, looking slightly sideways |
| Relatable / everyday | At the desk with mic and cracked mug |
| Confused / lost | Walking, not paying attention |

### ChatGPT / image AI prompt template
```
YouTube thumbnail, 1280x720px, acid yellow background (#D4FF00).

Left side: Episode title text in large bold condensed font — "[INSERT 3-5 WORD TITLE]" — black, slightly textured/grungy, not perfectly clean.

Right side: Cartoon illustration of a tall, bearded Indian man, round glasses with one cracked lens, messy dark hair, grey t-shirt, flat illustration style with bold outlines. Expression: [INSERT MOOD — mid-thought / confused / tired]. Pose: [INSERT POSE].

Bottom right corner: Small cracked oval mirror logo mark.

Style: Raw, unpolished, Bobby Lee energy. No gradients, no drop shadows, no extra decorative elements.
```

## Reel / Instagram Image

### Format
- 1080x1920px
- Vertical / portrait

### Two types of Reel images

**Type 1 — Quote card** (for standalone posts, not tied to an episode clip)
```
[Acid yellow or black background — alternate between episodes]

[One line from the episode script — large, bold, centered]
[Mascot — small, bottom right, semi-transparent watermark style]
[Show name — very small, bottom center]
```

**Type 2 — Episode promo** (posted same day as episode drops)
```
[Acid yellow background]

[Mascot — upper half, mid-thought pose]
[Episode topic in bold — center]
["New episode out now" — small, below]
[Cracked mirror logo — bottom right]
```

### Text rules for Reels
- One line only on the card. Not two. Not three. One.
- Pull the most relatable or funniest line from the script
- No hashtags on the image itself — those go in the caption
- Examples of good pull quotes:
  - "The kind of hope that doesn't know it's hope."
  - "Broken omelette. Every single time."
  - "Haan haan. I'm fine."
  - "He's two levels ahead. I found out on a Tuesday."

### ChatGPT / image AI prompt template

**For quote card:**
```
Instagram Reel image, 1080x1920px, [acid yellow / black] background.

Center of image: One bold line of text — "[INSERT QUOTE]" — large, condensed, [black / white depending on background], slightly rough texture on font.

Bottom right: Small cartoon mascot bust — bearded Indian guy, round glasses with one cracked lens, messy hair, flat illustration style, semi-transparent watermark style.

Bottom center: "Side Effects of Thinking" in very small text.

Style: Minimal, raw, no decorative elements, no gradients.
```

**For episode promo:**
```
Instagram Reel cover image, 1080x1920px, acid yellow background (#D4FF00).

Upper center: Cartoon illustration of tall bearded Indian man, round glasses with one cracked lens, messy dark hair, grey t-shirt, [INSERT POSE]. Flat illustration style, bold outlines, no complex shading.

Center: Bold condensed text — "[INSERT EPISODE TOPIC — 3-5 words]" — black, slightly textured.

Below text, small: "new episode out now" — lowercase, not bold.

Bottom right corner: Small cracked oval mirror logo mark.

No gradients. No drop shadows. Nothing extra.
```

## What To Always Include
- Mascot (full, bust, or watermark depending on format)
- Cracked mirror logo mark somewhere
- Acid yellow as primary background (can use black as alternate for variety)

## What To Never Include
- Stock photos
- Real photos of anyone
- Gradients or glows
- More than one focal text element
- Anything that looks like a motivational quote page
