# Why I'm Not an Atheist

Yesterday I was getting late for office.

So...

I jumped a traffic signal.

The moment I crossed it, I saw a cop standing there with a camera.

He couldn't stop me. I was already gone.

But from the moment I saw him, there was only one thought in my head.

**"Oh God... please don't let there be a challan."**

Because once that challan comes...

God knows what amount they'll write.

₹500?

₹1000?

₹10,000?

Then you'll spend more time arguing in court that lok adalat.

So the entire day...

"Hey Bhagwan... challan mat bhejna."

For two days I kept checking.

Nothing.

No challan.

My prayers worked.

God is with me.

Thank you for listening.

Goodbye.

...

Now keep that story in your mind.

Because I want to tell you another one.

---

October.

1930.

Lahore Central Jail.

A man is sitting inside a prison cell writing an essay.

Not a page.

Not two pages.

Nearly six thousand words.

He finishes writing it on the 6th of October.

On the 7th of October...

He is sentenced to death by hanging.

Five and a half months later...

he is executed.

He is twenty-three years old.

Six months after his death...

a weekly newspaper in Lahore called *The People* publishes that essay.

He never got to see it published.

The essay was called...

**Why I'm an Atheist.**

The man was Bhagat Singh.

---

The interesting part is...

he didn't write it because someone asked him to.

He wrote it because one of his own friends told him—

*"Popularity has gone to your head. Your ego has come between you and God."*

To which Bhagat Singh responded...

with a six-thousand-word essay.

My friends don't even reply with a 👍 emoji.

He wrote five thousand seven hundred and ninety words.

---

Now here's what i want to talk about
and let me just take the liberty to talk on behalf of believers. 
The smallest inconvenience happens to us...

and we pray.

Phone slips from our hand...

"Oh God..."

Exam result...

"Oh God..."

Interview...

"Oh God..."

Traffic challan...

Definitely...

"Oh God."

We don't even think.

We just pray.

But here is a man...

who knows a death sentence is only days away.

His friends ask him to pray.

Even the prison officers ask him to pray.

And he refuses.

He writes—

> *"I know in the present circumstances my faith in God would have made my life easier, my burden lighter and my disbelief in Him has turned all the circumstances too dry and the situation may assume too harsh a shape. A little bit of mysticism can make it poetical. But I do not want the help of any intoxication to meet my fate. I am a realist. "*

Later he writes—

> *"To stand upon one's own legs amid storms and hurricanes is not a child's play..."*

---

Now...

let's again talk about me.

I don't only pray when something bad happens.

I pray when I want something.

There is literally a document on my computer.

Three lines.

"Help me create things I'm proud of."

"Help my work reach the right people."

"Help me become better every day."

Who am I asking this help from ?

God.

Every single time I upload a video...

I immediately check the views.

The likes.

The shares.

And if they aren't there within the first hour...

I start praying.

"Please... let this one do well."

I also pray when something good happens.

Which...

hasn't happened in quite some time.

But hey...

"No news is good news."

So...

thank God for that.

---

I think I believe in God for two reasons.

The first...

because it helps me cope.

Bad news becomes a little less bad.

Fear becomes a little less scary.

The second...

because there is this voice in my head that says—

*"What goes around comes back around."*

Good.

Bad.

Whatever you do...

something somewhere keeps score.

If I had to rewrite that sentence...

I'd say—

**What goes around... God brings back around.**

---

Now here's the interesting part.

Bhagat Singh was willing to sacrifice his life for his country.

That was one struggle.

The other struggle...

was trying to live honestly as an atheist.


And here I am...

trying to keep a day job alive...

trying to keep this podcast alive...

trying to keep my sanity alive.

There's already enough going on inside my head.

So let me tell you what I mean when I say...

God.

Is this God divided by religion?

No.

Is this God omnipotent?

Maybe.

Maybe not.

I honestly don't know.

I just believe there is something outside me...

something I can place my faith in...

because it makes life a little easier to carry.

I choose to call that...

God.

---

Towards the end of the essay...

Bhagat Singh writes—

> *"For selfish motives I am not going to pray. Readers and friends, is this vanity? If it is, I stand for it."*

Me?

I'm nowhere close.

I pray to God...

at the drop of a hat.

So...

listeners and friends...

does that make me a coward...

or  human?


Whatever it is...

I stand by it.

---

Tell me what you think.

And tell me...

if I'm wrong.

Because...

my name is Prateek.

And this is...

**I Could Be Wrong.**

I'll see you in the next one.

Bye.
