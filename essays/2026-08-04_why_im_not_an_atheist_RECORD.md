October 1930. Lahore Central Jail. There is a man sitting in a cell writing an essay — six thousand words, essay.

He finishes the essay on the sixth of October.

On the seventh of October, the tribunal sentences him to death by hanging.

Five and a half months later, they hang him. He is twenty-three.

And six months after that, a Lahore weekly called *The People*  prints this essay.
This essay is called - 'Why I'm an atheist'
And this man is a 23 year old freedom fighter revolutionary named - 'Bhagat Singh'

He never saw it published. He'd been dead half a year.

Now as i mentioned he started writing it just one week before the sentence 

The trigger for him to write this essay was that one of his friend told him 
- Popularity has gotten into your head. Your ego is coming between you and god
