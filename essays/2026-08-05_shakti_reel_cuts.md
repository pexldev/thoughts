## REEL 1 — TYSON  (~45–55s) — the funniest, post this one first

**In:** "You are walking on the street"
**Out:** "On you - it just looks funny"

Full run:
> You are walking on the street / and you see Mike Tyson / And you slap his bald head
> Mike Tyson turns around and knocks the f**k out of you
> and people ask you why did you do that to Mike Tyson / are you nuts / are you ok
> and you say / its ok I forgive Mike Tyson
> Now tell me what sounds better / Mike Tyson forgiving you / Or / You forgiving Mike Tyson
> Forgiveness is a crown that looks good only on Mike Tyson / On you - it just looks funny

**Hook (first 2s):** the slap line. Don't let "You are walking on the street" sit alone — get to Mike Tyson inside 2 seconds or trim into it.
**Text card:** none needed at the top; the captions carry it. Punch up "I FORGIVE MIKE TYSON" as a big frame.
**Why it works standalone:** zero context required, complete argument, ends on a laugh.

## REEL 2 — THE COUSIN  (~50–60s)

**In:** "I have a cousin who at one point of time meant the world to me"
**Out:** "Dont go crying to mumma"

Full run:
> I have a cousin who at one point of time meant the world to me / and now he is just a name in my contact list
> He recently bought a house / and had a house warming / all of a sudden he calls / I did not pick up
> A guy who once blocked me is now inviting me to his housewarming
> Its like a rat inviting a King Cobra for a housewarming
> Then he makes his dad call me / Then he makes my dad call me / Then he makes my wife call me
> I'm still surprised they have each other's number
> He is 30 years old / If you are 30 years old / start dealing with your own sh*t / Dont go crying to mumma

**Hook (first 2s):** "A guy who once blocked me is now inviting me to his housewarming" is the stronger opener than the contact-list line — consider leading with it and dropping the cousin intro to one line.
**Big text frames:** the three-rung ladder (his dad / my dad / my wife) stacked on screen as it builds. That's the visual joke and it works muted.
**Why it works standalone:** universal — everyone has this cousin.

## REEL 3 — SEEDHE MAUT + DINKAR  (~40–50s) — the save-and-share one

Not a comedy cut. This is the one people send to each other.

**Structure (spliced — pulls from two places in the take):**

1. **HOOK — text on screen, no VO needed for the first beat:**
   > Mere paas do shashtra, main chunu kya — shakti ya kshama
   This is the Seedhe Maut catch. Fans stop scrolling on their own lyric.
2. **The thesis, in English** (from 77–79):
   > When people say you 'be the bigger person' / it usually means 'I know you have the power to crush him' / but just let it go
3. **Dinkar** (from 26–28):
   > Kshama shobhti us bhujang ko jiske paas garal ho / Usko kya jo dantheen, vishrahit, vineet, saral ho
   > Ramdhari Singh Dinkar. Rashmirathi. 1952.
4. **The chorus → the answer** (from 81–84), ending on:
   > Do mere shashtra hai shakti **aur** kshama
5. **CLOSING FRAME: YA struck through → AUR.** Non-negotiable. The whole thing dies without it.

**No music on this one.** Silence at the aur reveal.
**Why it works standalone:** the English thesis in the middle gives non-Hindi viewers stakes; the poem gives it weight; the YA/AUR frame gives it the click.
