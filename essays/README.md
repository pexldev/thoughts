# Daily essay practice

Started Aug 2 2026. The point is to find my voice on the page, where there's no punchline to hide behind.

**Why essays and not more jokes:** the lane I picked (Carlin / Louis CK register — authority, not loser costume) is the essayist's lane. Carlin wrote books. That voice gets built by writing prose until I know what I actually think, then adding the jokes on top. Not the other way round.

## The rules

1. **One essay a day. Dated file.** `YYYY-MM-DD_slug.md`
2. **30–45 minutes. Don't polish.** This is reps, not a product.
3. **Practice ≠ publishing.** Most of these never ship, and that's the point — the moment every essay has to become a video tomorrow, I start writing for the upload and the voice dies. Some ship. Most are reps.
4. **Write to one person**, not to an audience. (Same fix as the mic — see `prep/how_to_talk_to_mic.md`.)
5. **Read it out loud before calling it done.** This doubles as the mic drill. If a sentence is hard to say, it's badly written. Two birds.
6. **Same channel** if it ships. No new channels — splitting nothing into two nothings.

## Pre-flight check on every essay (my known tells)

- **Did I open with a thesis instead of a thing that happened?** My default is an abstract open. Start with the concrete grab — a scene, an object, a moment.
- **Did I close with advice?** My default is a sermon ending. Preaching is allowed now, but it has to be **paid for** — the sermon only lands after I've implicated myself. No scar, no sermon.
- **Did I flinch into stated emotion?** When it gets embarrassing I write "I felt something." Go back to the behaviour or the object. The uglier specific always wins.
- **Am I performing the loser?** Not my costume anymore. The self-implication stays, but the target changed: not *"I'm pathetic"* — *"I am precisely the thing I'm criticising."*

## Fact discipline

Anything historical, factual or attributed gets **checked before it's recorded**, not before it's written. Writing stays fast and loose; the camera doesn't. (Aug 2: the first draft had Bhagat Singh writing the essay one month before his hanging. It was five and a half. That one goes on camera and the comments eat me alive.)

Claude's job: run the check. Verified facts go in a block at the top of the essay file.

## Backlog

Ideas live in `ideas.md`. Each one needs a line on **why it itches** — if I can't say why it stuck to me, it isn't an essay yet.
