## STORY B PLAN (read this before building any scene)

The script's subtext: **nobody in this story thinks they're the villain — including the narrator.** He ends with "I sleep great." So the visuals run two silent threads that converge at the end:

**Thread 1 — THE HALO.** A glowing halo appears over every scammer *at the exact moment of their scam*. The opening frame plants it (everyone in a police lineup has one — unexplained). Manager trio gets theirs at the mute moment. The trainer gets his while sleeping on the money mattress. And in the final scene, as the mascot says "I sleep great" — a halo quietly fades in over HIS head. First-frame mystery, last-frame payoff. Viewers who rewatch will realize the mascot was standing in the lineup from second one.

**Thread 2 — THE ASTERISK.** Fine print is the actual villain of this episode ("sole discretion"). So an asterisk infects every written promise in the backgrounds: the office poster, the counter-offer email, the gym poster — and finally the show's own end card, which this one time reads **I COULD BE WRONG*** with microscopic fine print: *"subject to sole discretion."* The show scams its own logo. That's the screenshot frame.

**Egg sequence in order:** halo lineup w/ hidden scam math → "WE'RE A FAMILY*" poster → calendar invite "Optional: your future" → "HR is typing…" → asterisk hidden in the email → gym poster fine print → Google autocomplete → mascot's own halo → asterisked end card. Read in order, the props tell the whole episode without audio.
