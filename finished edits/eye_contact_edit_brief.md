# Edit Brief — I Could Be Wrong
## Episode: "Eye Contact"
### 26 Scenes (including Cold Open)

---

## Visual Style — Option B (apply to every frame)

- **Background:** Solid acid yellow (#F2FF00) fills the ENTIRE frame
- **Environment/props:** All location elements are flat black silhouettes — no fill, no detail, shapes only
- **Mascot:** The ONLY fully rendered element with color depth
- **Yellow coverage:** 70–80% of frame minimum
- **Exception:** Scene 25 (closing card) inverts to dark background + acid yellow text

---

## Mascot Expression Kit

- **Deadpan** — blank stare, default. Narration and setup.
- **Are You Serious** — one eyebrow up, looking slightly off-camera. Reacting to something absurd.
- **Spiral** — wide eyes, slightly vibrating outline. Anxiety and self-doubt.
- **Smug Roast** — half-smirk, chin slightly up. Roasting someone.
- **Am I Audible** — wide-eyed, panicked, mouth slightly open. WFH isolation moments.
- **Vulnerable** — eyes slightly down, shoulders dropped. Genuine self-reflection.

---

## Transition Guide (Canva)

| Transition | When to use |
|---|---|
| **Cut** (no transition) | Punchlines, jump cuts, cut-away gags, expression snaps |
| **Fade** | Emotional moments, same-location scene changes, pulling back after big moments |
| **Whip Slide** | Chapter breaks, new location reveals, energy shifts |
| **Shake Zoom** | Jump cut zoom into close-up — only for the two "AM I AUDIBLE" moments |
