# Edit Brief — I Could Be Wrong
## Short #2: "The Documentary" (FAFO — Taekwondo / Regret bit)
### 12 Scenes — Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts

---

## Visual Style — Option B (every frame)

- **Background:** Solid acid yellow (#F2FF00) fills the ENTIRE frame
- **Environment/props:** All location elements are flat black silhouettes — no fill, shapes only
- **Mascot:** The ONLY fully rendered element with color depth. Write as "The mascot" — no physical description spelled out.
- **Resolution:** 1080x1920 (vertical — portrait orientation)
- **Frame note:** Mascot sits center or lower-center in the tall frame. Props and text live above or flanking him.
- **Broken mirror logo:** Present on the dojo wall (Scene 2) and kitchen wall (Scene 10) — both have a wall in frame.
- **Post-launch CTA:** This Short drops after the full episode is already live, so the end card says "Full episode — live now," not a "coming Wednesday" teaser.

---

## Mascot Expression Kit

- **Deadpan** — blank stare, default. Narration and setup.
- **Are You Serious** — one eyebrow up. Reacting to something absurd.
- **Spiral** — wide eyes, vibrating outline. Anxiety, deflating.
- **Smug Roast** — half-smirk, chin up. Relief, narrator voice, final button.
- **Vulnerable** — eyes down, shoulders dropped. The fridge moment — the emotional core of this short.

| Transition | When |
