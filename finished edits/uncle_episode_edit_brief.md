## Visual Style — Option B (apply to every frame)

- **Background:** Solid acid yellow (#F2FF00) fills the ENTIRE frame
- **Environment/props/people:** All location elements AND secondary people (uncle, mom, dad, neighbor, son, doctor) are flat black silhouettes — no fill, no detail, shapes only
- **Mascot:** The ONLY fully rendered element with color depth. Write as "The mascot" — no physical description spelled out.
- **Yellow coverage:** 70–80% of frame minimum
- **Exception:** Scene 26 (closing card) inverts to dark background + acid yellow text
- **Broken mirror logo:** Present on any wall — studio backdrop, hospital room, and the dinner-table backdrop all qualify
- **Tone note:** This episode is mostly sincere narration with ONE big comedic detour (the sushi/diarrhea bit, Scenes 17–20). Don't let the comedic register bleed into the hospital/reveal scenes (12–14) or the closing reflection (21–22) — the contrast between registers IS the episode's point. Keep those scenes visually quiet: no shake-text, no big bold gags.

---

## Mascot Expression Kit

- **Deadpan** — blank stare, default. Narration and setup.
- **Are You Serious** — one eyebrow up. Reacting to something absurd (the uncle's casualness).
- **Spiral** — wide eyes, vibrating outline. The 2 AM panic, the "I'd write my will" overreaction.
- **Smug Roast** — half-smirk, chin up. Self-aware cringe, the press-conference bit.
- **Vulnerable** — eyes down, shoulders dropped. The reveal, the reflection, the close — the real spine of the episode.
