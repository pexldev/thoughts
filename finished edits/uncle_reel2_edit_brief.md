## Visual Style — Option B (every frame, matches Reel 1 / episode)

- **Background:** Solid acid yellow (#F2FF00) fills the ENTIRE frame
- **Environment/props/people:** All location elements and secondary people (uncle, son, doctor) are flat black silhouettes — no fill, shapes only
- **Mascot:** The ONLY fully rendered element with color depth. Write as "The mascot" — no physical description spelled out.
- **Resolution:** 1080x1920 (vertical — portrait orientation)
- **Broken mirror logo:** Not used here — every scene is a phone call, hospital corridor, or freestanding prop (calendar, hospital bed). No flat wall-backed sets in this reel.
- **Tone note:** This is a misdirection bit — the whole reel plays the "minor procedure" line straight and casual, then yanks the rug with the cornea transplant reveal. Keep Scenes 1–7 light and dismissive in tone. Scene 8 is the turn. Scenes 9–10 are the reveal and must read as a genuine gut-punch, not a punchline — no shake-text gimmicks until the reveal text itself.

---

## Mascot Expression Kit

- **Deadpan** — blank stare, default. Use for narration beats.
- **Are You Serious** — one eyebrow up. Reacting to the uncle's casualness.
- **Smug Roast** — half-smirk, chin up. Mimicking/quoting the uncle's dismissive lines.
- **Spiral** — wide eyes, vibrating outline. The moment the reveal lands.
- **Vulnerable** — eyes down, shoulders dropped. The beat right after the reveal settles.
