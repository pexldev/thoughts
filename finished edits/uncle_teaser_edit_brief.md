## Visual Style — Option B (every frame)

- **Background:** Solid acid yellow (#F2FF00) fills the ENTIRE frame
- **Environment/props/people:** All location elements and secondary people (uncle, mom, dad, neighbor) are flat black silhouettes — no fill, shapes only
- **Mascot:** The ONLY fully rendered element with color depth. Write as "The mascot" — no physical description spelled out.
- **Resolution:** 1080x1920 (vertical — portrait orientation)
- **Broken mirror logo:** Not used here — no walls/rooms with a flat wall surface in this teaser (hospital bed, table, doors, building exterior, podium are all freestanding silhouettes, not wall-backed sets)
- **Tone note:** This teaser swings between genuine and comedic. Scenes 1 and 6 are sincere — no jokes, no shaking text. Scenes 2–5 are the comedic contrast. Don't blur the two registers.

## Mascot Expression Kit

- **Deadpan** — blank stare, default. Narration and setup.
- **Spiral** — wide eyes, vibrating outline. The 2 AM panic.
- **Smug Roast** — half-smirk, chin up. The self-aware cringe and the press-conference bit.
- **Vulnerable** — eyes down, shoulders dropped. The opening line and the closing question — the two real moments in this teaser.
