## THUMBNAIL — 1 image, +1 (exempt from reuse) · **BLACK FLIP, his call 11 Aug**

**The principle, now locked:** *yellow is the inside of the episode, black is the doors.* The CTA end slide is the door out; the thumbnail is the door in. Same flip, same font weight — a stranger who saw the CTA card once recognises the thumbnail in a feed for free.

**The one risk, and the mitigation:** black works on the CTA slide because every frame around it is his yellow. In a YouTube grid, everything around him is *already* dark — so a black thumbnail forfeits some of the yellow moat. Fix: the yellow must be ONE ENORMOUS SHAPE, never scattered detail. Both concepts below are built that way. Compare CTR against the ep1–3 yellow thumbnails at the next Sunday read before making the flip permanent.

**Text — LOCKED (his call, 11 Aug):** `WHY I DON'T SAY BYE` — acid yellow, heavy condensed caps, top third, two lines. Baked into the prompt, never a Canva layer.

**Why it's the right one:** the title demands manners from everybody else; the thumbnail admits he has none. The self-implication is visible *before* anyone presses play — the whole episode in two strings. A thumbnail and a title have to argue, not nod.

⚠️ **Watch the apostrophe.** It's the most-mangled character in generated text — comes back as a comma, a tick, or a blob. Check it before accepting. If two rolls both botch it, switch to `WHY I NEVER SAY BYE` — same meaning, same length, nothing to break.

**Stamp position, thumbnails only:** the JOKE LAB badge sits **bottom-LEFT** here, not bottom-right. YouTube prints the video duration over the bottom-right corner and would bury the series mark every week.

**Superseded text options, for the record:** ~~`NOBODY SAID BYE`~~ (written against the old title, just agreed with it) · ~~`BYE IS A CRIME`~~ · ~~`SHE JUST LEFT`~~ — highest curiosity but it points the finger at her, and Beat 4 only works because he's the fool in it. Don't sell a bit he didn't write.
