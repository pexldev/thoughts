**Format note:** Ep4 is one threaded bit across six beats, not a 10-joke run. So a reel here is **one whole beat**, not a joke run — the two-beat setup/punch grammar still applies, it just maps to phases inside the beat instead of joke 1 / joke 2 / joke 3.

# ⭐ REEL 1 — "MANNERS" *(lead — make this one first)*

**VQ 10/10.** Highest score anything's hit on the scorecard. It's about the viewer's marriage, not his anecdote — that's the whole reason it travels.
**Send this to:** the person you fight with. Specifically the one who walks out mid-argument.
**Runtime:** ~38s ⚠️ *at the top of our 30–40s lane — if it comes in over 45, cut the permission list from three items to two ("be angry / call me an asshole"). Never cut the quiet room; the silence is what makes the anger funny.*

**Why it wins:** it's debate bait AND an identity badge at the same time. Half the comments will be "you're being unreasonable" and the other half "FINALLY someone said it" — and both of those people share it. The closer is a rule someone can adopt as vocabulary, which is our highest save-rate move.

**Send this to:** your sibling. This is a family-group-chat reel, and family/relationship material is our strongest share channel on paper (~60% female audience).

## SECOND TIER — cut only if the week needs volume

- **Beat 6, "just ghost them"** (VQ ~8) — very short, high identity-badge value ("this is so me"), and "just erase them from my life" is quotable. Loses points on escalation: it's one move, not a run. Strong candidate for a **face-to-camera** short rather than an animated one — it's a confession, and a face sells a confession better than a mascot.
- **Beat 1, the death rule** (VQ ~7) — best in-group flare in the episode (every Indian household has a version), but the punch is *curious* more than funny, and it's the episode's premise. Its job is to make people watch the long-form.
- **Beat 2, dad's tax** (VQ ~6) — needs the Form 16 setup to land and it's the most inside-baseball beat. Leave it in the episode.
- **Beat 5, the compounding debt** — do NOT cut as a reel. It's the beat that makes him look worst without a laugh big enough to pay for it, and it needs the episode around it.
