## What You're Recording

A 4-joke bam-bam run on the observation that nobody in India seems to use dustbins. Ends on a relationship punchline that hits twice — once for the country, once for your personal life. That's the closer. Everything before it is setup for that last line.

**Estimated VO runtime: ~29 seconds**
Full reel with hook frame + end card: ~33 seconds

## The Hook (on screen while you say it)

> **"People in this country just don't seem to see dustbins."**

Say it flat. No energy. That's the observation — the jokes earn the energy.

---

## What You Record (in this order)

---

**JOKE 1**

> "I once saw a guy in the park aiming a chocolate wrapper into the penguin dustbin's mouth. He missed. I told him 'It missed.' He said — 'Yeah I know. That penguin moved.'"

**Notes:**
- The beat before "That penguin moved" is the whole joke. Let it land.
- His delivery should be 100% sincere, zero irony. He genuinely believes the penguin moved.
- Fast in, fast out. Don't explain.

---

**JOKE 2**

> "I saw a guy in the airport bathroom wash his hands, wipe them with a tissue, and throw it in the sink. He had an Apple Watch, a Gabit ring... and somehow the dustbin was the smartest thing in the room."

**Notes:**
- The list (Apple Watch, Gabit ring) is the setup — each item signals this guy has money, taste, presumably a brain.
- "Somehow the dustbin was the smartest thing in the room" lands better if you say it like you're still processing it. Not a punchline delivery — a conclusion delivery.

---

**JOKE 3**

> "My dad throws squeezed lemon out of the window. 'The birds will eat it.' With that logic — if I throw my Form 16 out the window, will some CA pick it up and do my taxes?"

**Notes:**
- "With that logic" is the pivot. Slight pause before it.
- "Some CA" — the "some" matters. It's not A CA, it's some random CA who just happens to be passing. That's the absurdity.
- This is the family joke. People will share this to their parents. Deliver it affectionately, not annoyed.

---

**JOKE 4 — THE CLOSER**

> "I broke up with my ex because she was toxic. I'm still in touch with her."

*[beat — 1.5 seconds]*

> "Like my countrymen... I don't know how to dump trash."

**Notes:**
- The beat between those two sentences is the entire joke. Do NOT rush it.
- "I'm still in touch with her" — say this like you're confessing it. Not proudly, not self-pityingly. Just stating a fact you're slightly embarrassed by.
- "Like my countrymen..." — slow. Let the callback land.
- "I don't know how to dump trash." — this is the button. After you say it, stop. Don't laugh, don't move. Let the mascot hold it.
