## SHORT 2 — "We Won't Say Hi. But We'll Marry One." (~24s)

**HOOK (then straight into joke 1, no pause):**
In India, smiling at a stranger gives one of two vibes. You're selling something. Or you're a creep.

1. I was at Linking Road and a guy asked — "Aur bhaiyya, kaisa chal raha hai?" I said "Thik hoon bhaiyya, aap kaise ho?" He immediately said: "Belt dekhoge? Belt?" My brain now has a fourth entry.

2. If someone doesn't fall into any of those categories — I call them a USO. [beat] Unidentified Smiling Object.

3. I once said "you look beautiful" to a lady I passed. I watched her eyes go: WTH. [beat] "Jaa be tharki." [beat] ...ohh, maybe he's just complimenting me. And then her mouth said — "thank you." [beat] That thank-you had to clear three security layers.

4. **THE CLOSER:** Indians won't say hi to a stranger. [beat — 1s] But we'll marry one.

*(Land it small — the quieter, the harder it hits. Stop. Hold. End card.)*

## SHORT 3 — "He Looked Disappointed." (~29s)

**HOOK (deadpan, like reading a manual):**
This is how my brain classifies oncoming strangers.

1. If it's a guy in his 20s, wearing an I-card, and smiling — it's charity. [beat]
   If it's a guy in his 30s, wearing an I-card — it's a credit card. [beat]
   If it's a guy in his 40s, wearing his child — [longer beat]
   ...his wallet was stolen, his wife is pregnant, his mother had cancer, she died this morning, he just came from Tata Hospital... and all he needs... is ₹380 for a train ticket to go back home.
   *(20s/30s lines fast; slow down for the ₹380 speech — the runtime IS the joke.)*

2. I met a USO — a girl in her 20s, no I-card, but smiling. My brain panicked. Then she said: "Hello, don't worry — I'm not selling anything." My brain relaxed. [beat] Two minutes later, she was asking me to donate my eyes. [beat] She was right. She wasn't selling. Selling means I get something in return.

3. **THE CLOSER:** I saw the same girl again in the evening. Same spot. A guy was asking her — left eye, or right eye? She was explaining that the donation only happens after he's dead. [beat — 1.5s] He looked disappointed.

*(Say it like a police report. Zero commentary. Stop. Silence. End card.)*
