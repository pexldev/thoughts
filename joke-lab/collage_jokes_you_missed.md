## ⚖️ THE ONE-GLANCE LAW

Two slides failed generation before this law existed. Same fault both times, and it wasn't the joke:

- **Three competing text sizes.** Four lines at near-equal weight gives the eye no entry point. You read the *texture* of a joke, not the joke.
- **A prop behind the type.** "Low opacity silhouette behind the text" produced a muddy grey chair and a grey phone sitting under the words.
- **A four-line setup.** Nobody reads a paragraph on a thumb-scroll. The setup goes unread, so the punch lands on nothing.

**The law, every slide:**

1. **TWO text sizes. Never three.** Small setup, HUGE punch, ratio at least 1:3. If you can't tell instantly which is which, it's wrong.
2. **The huge block is 2–4 words** — the funny keywords, not the sentence. Big type is a spotlight, not a transcript.
3. **Setup is small, one or two short lines, 12 words max.** If it won't compress, it isn't a carousel joke — back to the bench.
4. **Nothing behind the type.** No prop, no low-opacity anything. The type IS the image.
5. **Bottom band holds two things, not three.** Mascot bottom-left, JOKE LAB stamp bottom-right. Nothing in between them.
6. **Size the mascot with a number, never an adjective.** "Small, in the corner" came back as half the frame with dead black beside him. Every prompt says **"no taller than one sixth of the frame height."** If he arrives bigger than the punch line is tall, regenerate.
7. **Cut setup words, never the frame.** A line that establishes *who is talking to whom* or *what attitude you're taking* is load-bearing — cutting it turns a joke into a caption. Only cut words that repeat something the reader already has. This law exists because cutting "I have a brilliant solution…" broke slide 4 and had to be reversed the same day.

**Mascot sizing:** big and staged on **slide 1 only** — it's the scroll-stopper, and a pure text card doesn't stop thumbs. Small bottom-left corner on **slides 2–4**, identical size and position every time, so he reads as a signature. Featured again on **slide 5**, because the CTA is his.

**No broken mirror logo.** The wall rule doesn't trigger — no walls here — and the JOKE LAB stamp is already the series mark. A third mark is clutter competing with the punch.

**The test:** hold the slide at arm's length, look for one second. If the huge words hand you the joke by themselves, ship it. If not, regenerate.

## GENERATION NOTES

- **One slide at a time.** Each prompt is ~20 words of baked text, which models render clean. Don't batch.
- **Slides 2–4 must be identical in layout** — same small-line position, same huge-block position, same corner mascot, same stamp. Only the words and the ground colour change. That sameness is what makes it read as a series instead of four unrelated posts.
- **Proofread every string character by character** before accepting. Baked text can't be patched.
- Nothing is added after generation. The stamp is in the prompt; nothing goes on in Canva.
