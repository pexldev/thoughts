# Nobody Watches The Demo

Recently I was flying to Kochi. And like everytime, the cabin crew was performing the safety demo, and like everytime, no one was paying attention to them.

But I pay attention to them everytime.

Now it's not because I'm a pervert — it's because if you actually pay attention, the safety demo is different every single time.

There are airlines that have a light on the life vest. There are airlines whose oxygen masks actually look like they work. Then there are airlines that have a whistle on the life vest.

Who am I supposed to whistle at when the plane crashes in the middle of the ocean? A shark? A dolphin?

Even the cabin crew have different styles.

Some are new — so they're really passionate about the demo.

Some are doing it for the 100th time. They know no one gives a fuck. So even they don't.

And then once I saw an attendant actually stop the demo and snap at some twenty-year-old on his phone —

*"Sir. Sir. SIR. You need to pay attention. Put your phone on flight mode."*

I've been flying since the time when attendants actually made the safety demo announcements themselves on the microphone. Now it's just the same recorded voice on every flight.

I think in a few years they would outsource the demo to some AI. Just ChatGPT saying,

*"In the unlikely event of an emergency, please ensure your own safety before assisting others…*

*…including your mother."*

---

I always prefer the emergency exit seats. And emergency exit briefing is like viva.

The attendant came to me and said:

*"Sir, in case of an emergency, are you willing and able to open this door?"*

"Yes."

Guy next to me —

"Yes."

*"Do you want me to repeat anything?"*

"No."

Guy next to me —

"No."

Brother did not hear a word. He is just copying my answers.

---

But sometimes I think about the cabin crew.

There must've been a time when that attendant was learning the safety demo for the first time. And there's a lot to remember. Seatbelt. Oxygen mask. Life vest. Emergency exits.

The flight is basically an escape room.

She would've practiced the shit out of it. Which means at some point, she was alone in her room, standing in front of a mirror, practising the safety demo.

And she must've been excited to perform for her first flight. She might've even fantasised —

*"I'll do it so well, the entire flight will give me a standing ovation."*

And then she finally does it…

and there's a baby crying in 14B…

and the entire flight is hooked to the baby.

She's performing in front of 180 people — and she's bombing.

I come here every week and bomb too. At least she has a baby crying in 14B.

I'm competing with every baby on the internet.
