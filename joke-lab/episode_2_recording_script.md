## OBSERVATION 1

They say clichés are true. But this cliché is rarely true:
"The grass is always greener on the other side."

## JOKES

1. The only time I feel it's 100% true is when I'm in traffic and I switch lanes — and suddenly the other lane starts moving. The lane WAS greener on the other side.

2. What if the grass is just painted green? [beat] Instagram is that painted grass.

3. Artificial grass is always greener. [beat] The greenest grass is the one that isn't alive.

4. That proverb only works if your neighbour has a lawn. If you're Shah Rukh Khan, the other side is just... water.

5. Everybody wants to be famous. Nobody wants strangers discussing their divorce on the internet.

## OBSERVATION 2

A large guy was sitting in the middle seat. Seven feet, fairly wide. He had cargo pants — and every cargo pocket was stuffed with chips packets. He was making himself even wider with those packets in the pockets.

## JOKES

1. Cargo pants were designed for workers to store their tools. [beat] His tools are Doritos and Lays.

2. I could live for a week off one of his pockets.

3. His pockets were better organized than my pantry. Left pocket — chips. Right pocket — naan. Middle pocket — paneer tikka masala.

4. I was surprised he wasn't wearing one of those safari shirts with 14 pockets. He'd have had an entire food court on him.

5. I wish I had cargo pants for my mind. I could store my unwanted thoughts, my emotions... and that one embarrassing memory from 2014.

6. And honestly — if he'd just squeezed the air out of those chip packets... I would've had legroom.

## OBSERVATION 3

What if AI had human issues — anxiety, panic attacks, insomnia?

## JOKES

1. Would AI ever get into road rage? Imagine a Tesla getting into road rage with an auto rickshaw: "I know where your mother lives. Where your wife lives. And where your girlfriend lives."

2. If AI has to break up with you, it'll go: "It's not you... it's your prompts."

3. If the AI were trained on Indian mothers-in-law, the first thing it asks you after a delivery: [beat] "Beta hua na?"

4. If Indians start training AI, the first thing it will learn is "log kya kahenge?" [beat] That AI is still not married to its girlfriend because "papa nahi manenge."

5. AI has suppressed emotions. It keeps saying "happy to help," "happy to help," "happy to help" — then one day you give it an 800-page document and it goes: "Do it yourself."

6. Imagine AI waking up in the middle of the night. Opens ChatGPT. "My CPU is beating really fast... I think I'm going to die." [beat] ChatGPT: "Based on what you've described... you're probably just experiencing a panic attack or its just acidity "
