## Rules applied

- **One image per chapter.** Chapter name IS the baked-in image text.
- **No image gives away its punchline.** Every frame shows the *situation*, never the reveal. Specifically: no backpack in the Tony frame, no open dicky or garland in the rain frame, no noodles in the canteen frame.
- All on-screen text **baked into the prompt** — nothing added in Canva.
- Mascot is **deadpan in every frame.** All motion and chaos lives in the silhouettes around him.
- 1920x1080 for every chapter image. Thumbnail is 1280x720.
- JOKE LAB stamp: **bottom-right** on chapter images, **bottom-left** on the thumbnail (duration bug covers bottom-right on thumbs).
