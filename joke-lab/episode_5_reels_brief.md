## ⚠️ ONE CHANGE BEFORE YOU RECORD — the hook

Scored as written, this reel is a **7/10** on the VQ scorecard. The single point it drops is the hook, and it's the cheapest fix in the file.

*"Sometimes when you are working from home…"* is a wind-up, which `ops/reels_that_work.md` lists as anti-pattern #1 — "swiped before the verb."

**The fix is one deleted word.** Open cold on the tenth-of-a-second image, then give the context:

> **~~Internally~~ There are 10 versions of you arguing over 100 life choices you made in past 6 months.**

Then the silence lines, then the button. Same words, reordered, one cut. Hook goes 1 → 2, **VQ 7 → 8.**

# REEL 2 — THE BASEMENT
**~36s · 5 images + CTA slide**

> **Note on a rule that looks reversed:** in the *episode* brief I hid the garland, the open boot and the backpack, because a chapter thumbnail sitting in a YouTube chapter list spoils a laugh that hasn't arrived yet. A reel is the opposite — the image IS the punchline's picture, and a muted viewer needs to see the joke, not wait for it. So here the boot opens on screen.

## ⚠️ Standalone problem — read first

This is the highest-ceiling reel in the episode and it has one real defect: **Tony is a stranger.** In the episode he's been built for four minutes; in a reel he arrives with no history, and "my best friend" is doing all the work in one line.

The opening line below is **assembled from your own words** (*"she's also talking to Tony"* + *"Tony was a good friend of mine, one of my best friends"*), compressed into one setup. It isn't a joke and I'm not claiming it as one — but it's your line only in the sense that I welded two of yours together. **Say it out loud and reword it in your mouth before you record.**

## THE VO — trimmed to ~36s

> My best friend Tony was also talking to my crush.
>
> One day it's pouring rain. Tony comes in his SUV, I'm standing there with my umbrella. And next to Tony sits my crush.
>
> She calls me to hop in.
>
> He drives us to his building basement. And he opens up his dicky.
>
> And in the dicky is decoration. A garland which said — will you be my girlfriend.
>
> Tony knew everything. He made her ask me to hop in so he could rub it on my face.
>
> I asked my crush — are you going to say yes?
>
> She said yes.
>
> *(pause — take it, this is the whole reel)*
>
> Then I said — congratulations guys. I'm so happy for you.

**Trimmed out:** "we go for a drive", "and then finally", "okay?" — connective tissue that costs seconds and buys nothing. **Cut on "happy for you."**
