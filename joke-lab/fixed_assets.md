### ⚠️ Two things to get right at generation, because you only do this once

**1 · The JOKE LAB stamp moves to the TOP-right on this card only.** On every other Joke Lab frame it lives bottom-right — but Instagram's caption block, audio ticker and action buttons cover the bottom ~15% and the right edge of a reel. A bottom-right stamp on a card whose whole job is being read would sit under the UI. Keep all copy inside the middle 70% of the frame vertically.

**2 · Check the letters harder than usual.** Baked text can't be corrected without a full regenerate, and this asset ships on every reel from now on — a typo here is a typo on everything. Read "I COULD BE WRONG" character by character before you accept it. The upside: it's a one-time cost, and both strings are short enough to come back clean.
