## INSTAGRAM — one static, 1080x1350 (4:5)

**Colour:** scene colour Chai brown (#7A4B2A) — the parents' house. Yellow anchor **C — yellow hero object** (the glass).

**Why C:** the glass is the joke. Making it the only blazing thing in a brown room means the eye lands on the object before it reads a single word.

**Do NOT** shape the sediment like a person. The image must stay an honest glass — if the picture makes the pun, the text has nothing to land on and the joke dies before it's read.

## What this post is actually testing

Not reach — a static won't travel past existing followers. It's a **share test.** Every reader can name the uncle, and nobody gets in trouble for forwarding it. Grade it on sends and saves, not likes. If it doesn't get sent, the joke is weaker than the room thinks.
