# adjust kar lo

**Vein:** sayings taken apart
**Premise:** "Adjust" is an engineer's word smuggled into a sentence about a human being. It's always issued to exactly one person — and the one saying it is exempt from it.
**Mechanism:** sense pun / literal break — the word does two jobs, and the second job is the accusation.

---

## KEEPER — 23 Aug 2026 (spar)

> "Adjust kar lo yaar," says my mechanic, every time he gives back my car.
> I gave him my car to fix the fan belt. Now I have fan belt noise and an adjusted door.

Rules that made it work: the word appears exactly twice — once in his mouth, once in yours. Setup stays innocent. Punch word last. No clause explaining the verdict.
