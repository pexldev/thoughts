## Level 3 – Thesis
- Human beings are terrible at appreciating what they already have.
- We overvalue what we don't have and undervalue what we do have.
- Comparison creates imaginary scarcity.
- The mind is designed to notice missing things, not existing things.
- The other side doesn't have to be better—it just has to be unavailable.
