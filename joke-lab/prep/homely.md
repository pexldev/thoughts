# HOMELY
*Sparred 24 Aug 2026. Word given, joke found by him. Lines are his, verbatim.*

## The word
**"Homely"** — the matrimonial-ad word that means beautiful in India and ugly in English. Somebody chose that word on purpose.

## The bit (v1 — shipping version)

> My dermat says there is only one cure to my tan
> avoid direct sun exposure.
> im now a homely husband
> My wife goes to work she is a well-settled husband

## Why it works
- **The mechanism is his:** a *dermatologist* delivering a verdict in **biodata vocabulary**. Medical authority, matrimonial grammar. Straight-faced clinical register on an emotional subject — his signature move.
- **"Tan"** does the colorism work without the word *fair* ever appearing. The audience supplies it.
- **"Homely husband"** = he pins a word women get graded on onto himself. Self-implicating by default; nobody in the joke is the victim except him.
- **The tag doesn't switch the gender, it switches the GRADE.** She isn't "a typical husband" (generic) — she's **well-settled**, the ads' word for men, which measures salary and pretends to measure character. Two people, two biodata words, both misfiled.
- Punch word last in both beats.

## Vocabulary that carried it
- *avoid direct sun exposure* — dermats say **exposure**, not "going outside." It turns a person into a surface.
- *well-settled* — a bank statement wearing a virtue.
