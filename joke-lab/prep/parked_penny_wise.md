## The discovery (the whole bit lives here)
- Original complaint: wife saves ₹2 on detergent at DMart but spends 25k on shoes "she could buy for 3k first copy, ditto same."
- **The turn: "first copy ditto same" was never advice for her — it's MY shoe philosophy. I own a wardrobe of first copies.** She's a woman who owns real shoes; I'm a man who owns costumes of shoes.
- Deeper layer: I have STANDARDS within fakes — "not the expensive original, but not the very cheap fake either." A connoisseur of counterfeits. Somewhere there's a man whose fakes I look down on.
- Frame that works (Burr — bleed first): open judging her, land on me being the bigger fraud. She overpays for real things; I pay real money to fake having money.
