# Topic: Petty Revenge

The cliché everyone repeats: *"an eye for an eye will make the whole world go blind."* Everyone nods. Nobody is blind. Because nobody is actually taking an eye — we've all downgraded to revenge so small the other person never finds out it happened.

Your line, 12 Jul, `ideas.md`:
> *"I'm not the guy who wishes ill on someone. But i have my own way of taking revenge hahahhha im evil that way."*

That sentence is the whole bit. The laugh is already in it — you said "I'm not the guy who" and then immediately told on yourself.

---

## Working Thesis

> **Thesis (draft):** I'm not a good person, I'm a coward with a filing system.
>
> **Thesis (locked):** I don't want justice. I want the other person to lose something so small they can't prove it and I can't be blamed for it. An eye for an eye is too expensive — I take an eye for a delayed reply.

**The prediction being broken:** this is a bit about a nice guy surrounded by vengeful people.
**What it actually is:** he's the vengeful one. He's just engineered his revenge down to a size that keeps the good-guy card in his wallet.
**The human tendency:** we don't forgive people. We downgrade the sentence until it's deniable, then call that being the bigger person.
**⭐ Where it lands on you (this is the closer, not a bonus):** the revenge is so small that the other person never notices — which means you are the only person serving the sentence. You're doing the time for a crime nobody registered.

**Temperature check vs ep6:** ep6 is *getting caught*. This one is *never getting caught, and that being worse.* Different engine — keep it there. Do not write this warm ("we all do it, isn't it funny"). It's you admitting you're small.

**⚠️ One-breath test, passed:** *"I never wish anything bad on anyone. I just take revenge in ways nobody notices."* Say that to a friend, they laugh and immediately tell you theirs. That's an ep4-grade premise.

## Level 1 – Obvious Questions

- If an eye for an eye blinds the world, what does *my* version blind? Nothing. What's the actual unit I trade in?
- Who decided revenge had to be big? Where did we get the idea that it's daggers and not seating arrangements?
- What if I took "an eye for an eye" completely literally — what would the paperwork look like? Who verifies the eye?
- What am I actually protecting when I keep the revenge small — them, or my reputation as the nice one?
- Who does this NOT apply to? (People who just say the thing out loud. What are they like? Are they happier? Terrifyingly, yes?)
- If aliens watched an Indian family being polite to someone they hate, what would they conclude the smiling means?
- How long is the sentence? At what point does the person get released, and who tells them?

## Level 2 – Pattern Questions

### Hack 1 — Where else have I seen this? ⭐ *(this level is the longest — go wide, one line per territory)*
- The WhatsApp group you refuse to leave, because leaving is a statement. So you mute it. Forever. That's the punishment.
- Weddings: you go, you're seen, and there's something you deliberately don't do while you're there. What is it in your version?
- The reply that could have taken four seconds and took two days. On purpose. What was on the phone screen while you didn't reply?
- The relative you're perfectly warm to on the phone, and what happens the second the call ends.
- Money someone owes you. Small enough that asking would be embarrassing, big enough that you have not forgotten the number.
- Someone cuts the queue / cuts you off in traffic — what do you actually do? (Not what you imagine doing. What your body does.)
- Neighbours. The building group. The lift. Where's the punishment in a shared lift?
- At work: the thing you technically did correctly, exactly as asked, knowing it would cost them. Malicious compliance has an Indian corporate flavour — find it.
- Your wife / your parents. What's the revenge that's so small it's basically a hobby?
- The bad restaurant, the bad barber, the bad auto driver — what do you take from them instead of confronting them? (A star rating is a weapon nobody calls a weapon.)

### Hack 2 — What's the verb here?
- The verb isn't *punish*. What is it? Withhold? Downgrade? File? Postpone? Pick the ugliest true verb — the whole bit's language comes from it.
- Whatever the verb is, where ELSE do people do that verb — banks, courts, HR, the government?

### Hack 3 — If I replaced the nouns, would the sentence still be true?
- "I never wish anything bad on anyone, I just ______" — who else in the world talks like this? A government? An insurance company? A landlord? Somebody's mother?
