## Jokes

1. **[DONE — sparred 22 Jul, his words]** "Any friendship starts with an acquaintance. But the true test of that friendship is how many reels you send the other person before you finally give up. In my case — it's three. If you're my friend, I'll send you three reels. If you don't react to any of those three reels — well, I'll stop sending you reels. And... why are you even my friend if I can't send you reels?"
   - PUNCH = the inversion (HE found it): friendship isn't for connection; the friend is a receptacle for his reels — no landing spot, no friend. Self-turn = his absurd transactional definition of friendship IS the confession.
   - Structure: understatement/anticlimax beat ("well, I'll stop sending you reels" = trivial punishment, deadpan) → inversion BUTTON ("why are you even my friend if I can't send you reels"). NOTHING after the button.
   - Kept "react" not "reply" (one thumb-tap, smaller = funnier). Read-aloud: "three" lands 3x — thin one at the mic.
   - UNUSED ALT punch (in pocket): the "one 🔥 react would've saved a 15-year friendship, you couldn't clear a bar that low" smallness/disproportion angle.
   - 🎬 frame: the mascot solemnly tapping "send" on a third reel into a silent chat, then closing the app like signing a death certificate — deadpan.
