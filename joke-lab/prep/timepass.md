# TIMEPASS
*Sparred 24 Aug 2026. Word given, joke found by him. Lines are his, verbatim.*

## The word
**"Timepass."** A whole country invented a word for activity whose only purpose is to make time go away. Nobody says it sadly. What it accidentally admits: time is the enemy and you are killing it.

## The bit (v1)

> My mom always looks at the clock and tells me 'aise timepass krne se acha kuch padhle'
> One day I looked at the clock and asked him 'aise timepass krne se acha kuch padhle'

## Why it works
- **The accusation gets redirected at the object** — and the object is the only thing in the room that literally does nothing but pass time. The clock is guilty of the exact crime.
- **Same sentence twice.** No new words in the punch; the turn is entirely in *who it is aimed at*. Cheapest possible construction, hardest to write.
- **"asked him"** — the clock gets a gender. Small, keep it.
- Hindi used correctly per the voice rule: a **quoted artifact**, mom's actual line, never translated. The narration around it stays English.
- Mom looking at the clock plants it in the room without announcing it.

## Route not taken
- "Today I was so swamped with work that day was just timepass" — work joke, dead under the corporate moratorium (thru ~21 Sep). Also asserts an opposite instead of turning.
- "My grandfather passed away. His clock is now timepass" — pun on *passed / timepass*. Sound, not meaning. Killed. (The image underneath — a dead man's clock still keeping time for nobody — was never cashed. Parked.)
