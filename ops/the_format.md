# THE FORMAT
**Locked 8 Sep 2026.** One channel. One thing. Open this before you write anything.

*This exists because the same decision was made on 25 Aug, lived in a chat, and was gone in ten days.*

## THE CHANNEL

**I Could Be Wrong.** That's the only name that appears in public.

No "Joke Lab #5". No "A Corporate Sermon". No "Wednesday". A franchise name only pays when there's an audience big enough to recognise it — at 10 subscribers it's three confusing labels on one shelf. **Titles are just titles.**

*The names stay in the repo — folders, prep files, skills. The system keeps working; the viewer just never sees it.*

## THE SHAPE — every episode, no exceptions

> ### DOOR
> A saying, or something everyone has witnessed. **Never your life.**
>
> ### MIDDLE
> Jokes hung off the objects inside the door. **One engine, many objects** — not a new angle per joke.
>
> ### ROOM
> You show up. Last thirty seconds. **Once. Unexplained.**

**Why this shape and not another:** karm karo is the only thing you've made that did 15x. It's a saying everyone's family repeats, taken apart, landing on *"1 view — my own view."* That's Joke Lab and Wednesday in one piece. You built the hybrid once by instinct and then spent six weeks trying to invent something else.

**The ratio matters.** karm karo spent thirty seconds on the *fal* and four words on himself. The confession is the **landing**, never a section — Wednesday is slow, this is fast, and a confession section makes the episode sag.

## THE GATE — before a word gets written

> **1. Can a viewer name someone to send this to?**
> **2. Are they *allowed* to send it?**

Both yes, or it doesn't get written. **The second question is the corporate lesson** — 0 shares in 28 days, because nobody can forward an office joke to anyone in their life.

## ALLOWED

- **Sayings taken apart** — the lead vein (karm karo = 245, the only 15x)
- **Witnessed observations** — a thing the viewer has personally sat through
- **Fiction at real-memory texture** — the thesis must be true, the anecdote needn't be
- **Hindi as a quoted artifact only** — *log kya kahenge*, *karm karo* — never translated, never narration
- **3–4 break-character asides per observation** — *"ohh that's a bad one"*, *"my dad will kill me"* — written on the page, not found at the mic
- **The self-turn — MANDATORY.** At least one joke at your own expense, every episode
- **Corporate as a LANDING** — the day job can implicate you in the last thirty seconds. It can never carry an episode.

## NOT ALLOWED

- **Your own life as the door.** It's the landing. Always.
- **"Nobody watches me" as a door.** Landing only — otherwise it's a complaint, not a spine.
- **The loser-comic posture.** Apologising for the bit mid-bit: *"anyways, I had this thought, thought I'd put it out there."* You're playing authority, not a guy who can't catch a break.
- **Sermon or advice closes.** Permission or self-implication, never instruction.
- **Corporate as a door or a middle.** Fails gate question 2.
- **Naming Deloitte.** Swap to KPMG / PwC / EY / Accenture.
- **Sub-format names in public titles.**
- **Text-card reel openers.** Frame one is a person, a face, or motion. Both platforms killed the text card.
- **Starting a new format before 8 episodes exist.** See the lock.

## THE HOOK — the first 20 seconds

**The number that sets every rule below: your channel's average view duration is 22 seconds.**

That is not a warning, it's a measurement. **Any joke that lands after 22 seconds is currently being heard by nobody.** Every craft argument about openings collapses into that one fact.

### The clock

| Time | What has to have happened |
|---|---|
| **0:00–0:03** | A person or a motion. Not a topic, not a preamble, not you. |
| **by 0:10** | The observation stated **cold** — the scorecard's own rule. |
| **before 0:22** | **The first laugh has landed.** Non-negotiable. |

### Banned openings — no wind-up, ever

- ❌ *"I was flying to my sister's place in Kochi…"* — travelogue
- ❌ *"And I noticed this one thing…"* — announcing an observation instead of making one
- ❌ *"I'm going to be honest…"* — hedge
- ❌ *"Let me tell you about…"* / *"So the other day…"* — permission-asking
- ❌ *"Hello everybody / how we doing"* — crowd energy at one person in earphones

**Open ON the thing.** She stands up. The mask drops. The uncle says the line. Then the observation. Then the first laugh, inside twenty seconds.

*Reference — the corporate sermon didn't open on "meetings are pointless." It opened on **"that meeting that you have 1.30 PM today."** The time did the work.*

## JOKE DENSITY — how often something has to pay

**The rule you already wrote:** *every minute pays in a laugh or in blood; a minute that pays in neither is a TED talk.* That's the **floor**, not the target.

**The target, because attention drifts about once a minute:**

| Spec | Number |
|---|---|
| **Laugh attempt** | every **~30 seconds** |
| **Minimum payment** (laugh *or* confession) | every **60 seconds** — hard floor |
| **In script lines** | a punch every **3–4 lines**. A setup is one sentence; two-sentence setups are stories. |
| **Per 8–12 min episode** | **20–25 attempts**, across 2 observations (~10 each) |
| **Word count** | **~1,300–1,900 words** (measured off the corporate script: 880 words = 5:27, ≈160 wpm) |

**Never three same-level jokes in a row** — that reads as a plateau even in a lab. Runs escalate, hardest last.

**Density is safe.** They can rewind; they cannot un-swipe. **Under-writing is the bigger risk than over-writing** — your own standard, and the 22-second number is what it looks like when it's ignored.

**The mortar:** 3–4 break-character asides per observation — *"ohh that's a bad one"*, *"my dad will kill me"* — 1–2 seconds, thrown away, not performed. Written into the script, not found at the mic. **Not after every joke**; that becomes its own pattern. Place them after the weird, personal, or risky ones.

**Bridges are one breath.** *"And the other thing about this…"* The glue between jokes is the shared **opinion**, not a segue. Written in his own mouth-words at assembly.

**8 episodes before the format question may be reopened.**

## THE TWO SCOREBOARDS — never let one grade the other

| **CRAFT** | **REACH** |
|---|---|
| breaks per minute · does it land on tape · zero dead positions at the close | shares · sends · swipe-away |

Craft is good and reach isn't there yet. Those are different problems with different fixes. **Do not read a reach number as a verdict on the writing.**
