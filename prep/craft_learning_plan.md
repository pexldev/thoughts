## What the show actually is — decided 15 Aug

Not a podcast. A podcast is companionship. What I make has no guests, no conversation, no news — just bits with setups, turns, tags, callbacks, punch word last.

> ### It's an hour, written in public. The episodes are the workshop. The reels are the door.

I named it right before I knew what it was: the workshop is called a **Lab**.

100 episodes isn't 100 pieces of content. It's the material bank for the hour.

**No stage, by choice, as of today.** That's a decision, not a gap — and it's fine, because the skill I'd be missing (reading a live room, riding a laugh) is a skill my format doesn't use. Recorded audio for one person in headphones is a different instrument.

## THE TWO SCOREBOARDS — never let one grade the other

| | **Is it good?** | **Did it reach anyone?** |
|---|---|---|
| Product | The material (bits) | The channel (reels/shorts) |
| Measured by | Breaks per minute · zero positions at the close · survives the cut test | Shares · watch-through · swipe-away |
| Where it lives | Episodes = the workshop | Reels = the door |

> **An 8-view episode with a great bit in it is a CRAFT SUCCESS and a DISTRIBUTION FAILURE.**
> Two different problems, two different fixes. Confusing them is what makes people quit.

## The missing signal (the real hole, and it isn't the stage)

A joke that has never met a human is untested. Three laugh-signals available without leaving the house:

1. **Shares.** A share is the internet's laugh — not a proxy for one, *is* one. Already tracked (ep1 reels ≈ 27% share-to-like).
2. **Delayed tape.** ⭐ Listen to my own recording **a week later, cold**, and mark every place *I* check out. A week is long enough to forget I wrote it. This is what Carlin actually did, obsessively, for decades. **This is the delivery instrument** — ep1 read flat and list-like and I could not hear it live. Nobody can. That's why the tape gets listened to on purpose, not in passing.
3. **One human, once, before recording.** As an instrument, not promotion. They don't have to laugh — I need to hear where they go quiet.

## The operating rule

> **Study is parasitic on shipping, never parallel to it.** One move per week, practiced in that week's Joke Lab bit.
>
> **Hard guardrail: if it's Thursday and the bit isn't written, the week's study is skipped.** Recording wins, always. Study that delays a recording is procrastination in a nice shirt.

## The weekly unit — 4 steps, ~2 hours

| Step | What | Time |
|---|---|---|
| **1. WATCH** | One bit — not a whole special — with **one thing to COUNT**. Counting, not admiring. | ~20 min |
| **2. STEAL** | The mechanism in one line, my words, into the card library. | ~10 min |
| **3. APPLY** | That week's bit uses the move **at least twice**, marked in the prep file. | (already doing it) |
| **4. CHECK** | One line into lab_notes after recording: did it land? | ~5 min |

Step 3 is the point. The rest is setup for it.

## THE SPINE: tight writing = the CUT

Carlin isn't tight because he wrote tight. **He's tight because he cut.** Nobody's first draft is Carlin. Everything below is built on that.

**The five drills, in order of value:**

1. **Hand-transcribe 60 seconds** of Carlin a week. Then try to remove one word without breaking it. Highest-return writing exercise that exists; nobody does it because it's boring.
2. **The 15% cut pass.** Every finished bit: remove 15% of the words without losing a beat. Not optional, not "if it feels long." A forced constraint is what produces economy.
3. **Read it out loud before it's done.** Every sentence I stumble on is badly written, not badly read.
4. **One-sentence setups.** Two sentences means it's a story, not a joke. Already in my template — now enforced.
5. **Kill the four gap-fillers** every pass: explaining, stated emotion, announcing, words after the punch. ([what_is_funny.md](what_is_funny.md))

**Delivery drills (solo, at the mic):**
- The **delayed tape** listen, above. Non-negotiable — it's the only ear I have.
- Asides written INTO the script, 3–4 per observation (lab_notes Note 1).
- Take the pause. Don't describe it, don't fill it.
