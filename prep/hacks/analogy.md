# Analogy Generation

---

## Hack 1 — "This is basically..." ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Finish the sentence:

> **This is basically a ________.**

Examples:

- Cargo pants are basically a pantry.
- YouTube is basically an adult pacifier.
- Corporate is basically high school with salaries.
- EMIs are basically renting your future.

---

## Hack 2 — Same Verb ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

Find the verb. Then find everything else that shares it.

**Example:** Cargo pants *fill.*

Where else do things fill?

- Hard drives
- Houses
- Calendars
- Stomachs
- Garages

Anything sharing the same verb can become an analogy.

---

## Hack 3 — Nature

Ask: *Does nature do this?*

- Squirrels store nuts.
- Ants hoard food.
- Camels store fat.

Now compare humans.

---

## Hack 4 — Technology

Ask: *If this were software, what would it be?*

- Overthinking = infinite loading screen
- Marriage = long-term subscription
- Memory = browser cache
- Cargo pants = external storage

---

## Hack 5 — Business

Ask: *If this were a company...*

- Cargo pants = warehouse
- Airport = shopping mall with planes
- HR = PR department for management

---

## Hack 6 — Child

Ask: *How would a 5-year-old describe this?*

- Cargo pants = pants with cupboards
- Elevator = standing car
- Tie = neck ribbon

Kids naturally create analogies.

---

## Hack 7 — Alien ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Ask: *If an alien saw this...*

> "This species stores potato slices on its legs."

Instant fresh perspective.
