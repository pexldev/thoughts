# Pattern Recognition

There's one hack responsible for 80% of good pattern recognition.

Stop asking **"Why?"**

Start asking: **"Where else have I seen this?"**

---

## Hack 1 — Where else have I seen this?

Take the cargo pants example.

**Observation:** Cargo pockets full of chips.

Now ask: *Where else do people fill empty space?*

- Bigger house → more furniture
- Bigger hard disk → more movies
- Bigger salary → more expenses
- Bigger suitcase → more clothes
- Bigger fridge → more food

Suddenly you've found a pattern.

---

## Hack 2 — What's the verb?

Forget the nouns.

Fat guy. Cargo pants. Lays.

Instead ask: *What is actually happening here?*

**Filling.**

Now your brain searches for everything else that involves filling.

That's pattern recognition.

---

## Hack 3 — If I replaced the nouns, would the sentence still be true?

> People fill cargo pockets.

↓ People fill calendars.  
↓ People fill bank accounts.  
↓ People fill garages.  
↓ People fill inboxes.

Now you've found a human behavior.

---

## The Three Questions

Memorize just these:

1. **Where else have I seen this?** ⭐ *(favorite)*
2. **What's the verb here?**
3. **If I replace the nouns, is this still true?**

These three questions alone will generate most of the patterns you're looking for.

The more reps you do, the faster your brain answers them automatically.
