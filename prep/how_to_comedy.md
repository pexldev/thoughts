## Mechanics 
Every joke is built from one or more comedy mechanisms.

| Mechanism          | Meaning                             | Cargo pants example                             |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **Contrast**       | Opposites collide                   | Cargo pants for tools → carrying chips          |
| **Incongruity**    | Something doesn't belong            | Snacks stored in clothing                       |
| **Exaggeration**   | Push reality further                | Enough chips to feed a village                  |
| **Literalization** | Take a phrase literally             | "He's wearing his groceries."                   |
| **Misdirection**   | Audience predicts A, gets B         | "Those are tools..." → "...for getting fatter." |
| **Irony**          | Opposite outcome                    | Bigger pockets make him less mobile             |
| **Character**      | Funny because of who someone is     | The prepared snack guy                          |
| **Absurdity**      | Logic stretched just beyond reality | Portable canteen                                |
| **Escalation**     | Keep increasing the idea            | Chips → candy → naan → refrigerator             |


## Techniques

### Semantic Deconstruction

Semantic deconstruction is where you take a word or phrase everyone accepts without thinking... and pull it apart until people realize it makes no sense.

The structure is usually:

    Word/Phrase → What does it actually mean? → Does that meaning hold up? → If not, expose the absurdity.

For example, Carlin:

    "Crippled people are not 'differently-abled.'"

He doesn't just disagree.

He asks:

    What does differently mean?
    What does abled mean?
    If everyone is able to do some things and not others...
    then everyone is differently-abled.

He deconstructs the phrase until it collapses under its own logic.

Mitch Hedberg does tiny semantic deconstruction

    "An escalator can never break. It can only become stairs."
