# How to structure a Wednesday script
<!-- Reverse-engineered Jul 13 2026 from the ep18 build, when I asked "why couldn't I structure it myself?" This is the answer as a reusable card. Run it before every episode. -->

## The reframe

**Structure is not writing. It's SORTING.** When I build an episode I've usually already written every line — as raw dumps, out loud, in asides. The work left is deciding *what goes where*. Generating and sorting are two different brains and **they cannot run at the same time.** The brain that produces the gold is deaf to structure by design. That's not a weakness — it's why editors are a separate job. My gap isn't a writing gap. It's that I never deliberately put the EDITOR hat on for a second pass.

## The one principle under everything

Every structural call answers one question:
**"What does the audience know right now, and what's the most powerful next thing to reveal?"**

- Generator brain asks: *what do I want to say?*
- Structurer brain asks: *what should they hear next?*

Structure = choreographing information so each reveal hits at max force.

## The six sorting moves (with the ep18 example that taught each)

1. **Wait-WHAT goes FIRST** — even if I said it in the middle or discovered it last. (Cold open = "I don't hide anything… actually 2 things." In the 901 draft the shock was buried at line 41.) Impact order, never lived/chronological order.

2. **Ration the best ammo — fire ONCE, at the peak.** (The 1-view stat. I even said "it's a crutch, I'm applying it everywhere." Banned it everywhere, saved one shot for the numbers confession.) A stat used twice is wallpaper.

3. **Plant early, harvest late — the distance IS the joke.** ("2-day play" = my fear in the rewind, then her mouth 3 minutes later in the taunt run = callback. Too close together = nothing.) Structure manages the gap between a plant and its payoff.

4. **Group REAL vs IMAGINED so the gap can land.** (Her real quotes — "do it, don't drag me in" — early, they read as fair. Her cruel quotes — dog audit — late, they read as my fiction. The gap between fair-her and cruel-her IS the thesis.) Interleave them and the contrast dies.

5. **The self-turn goes LATE.** (The gotcha-photo confession comes after all the fun of hiding, never before.) Front-load the story, back-load the introspection. Introspection-first kills episodes (Law 5 / the 12_other_guy flop).

6. **Meaning goes LAST and disguised.** ("Tatti person" — deepest point, dead last, delivered as a wound not a lesson.) Never open with the meaning. Land it as a wound or an open question.

## My two personal gaps (the honest diagnosis)

1. **I tell stories the way I LIVED them (chronological, context first) — scripts need HEARD order (impact first).** My natural opener is "it was a mix of feelings, it was this feeling…" and the gold arrives after the throat-clearing. Cut the runway, lead with the punch.

2. **I step over my own best lines as throwaway asides.** Real examples I nearly lost on ep18 night: "she doesn't know we can write scripts in VS Code," "I will send her this exact video," and "gun and bullets" (said as an *explanation*, not knowing it was the whole thesis). The generating brain is deaf to its own gold — that deafness is permanent, which is why a second pass (or a sparring partner) exists to catch it.

## The solo method — two passes, NEVER at once

- **PASS 1 — GENERATE.** Dump everything, ugly, out of order, no judgment. Do NOT structure while dumping. (Already my strength.)
- **PASS 2 — SORT.** Walk away. Come back as a stranger with the editor hat on. Run this checklist on the pile:
  - Where's my wait-WHAT? → rip it to the very top.
  - What's my ONE best stat/device? → ration it, one shot, at peak stakes.
  - What setups have payoffs elsewhere? → pull them apart, maximize distance.
  - What's real vs imagined/hypothetical? → group them, sequence for contrast.
  - Where does blame turn onto me? → that's the self-turn, push it late.
  - What's the meaning? → bottom, disguised, as a wound or an open question. Never stated flat, never in the middle.

## The encouraging truth

On ep18 night I already did ~40% of the sorting without noticing: recast the villain parents→school myself, ran the preach-test on my own line ("this is deep and preachy, let me turn it on myself"), self-caught the 1-view crutch, picked the rap-diary flip. The structural muscle is already firing. What's missing is doing PASS 2 **on purpose** instead of by accident.
