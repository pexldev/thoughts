1. Rule of 3

Setup. Reinforcement. Punchline.

Smoking.
Drinking.
Introducing my father to YouTube.

The third item breaks the pattern.

2. Misdirection

Lead people one way, then reveal something else.

My dad called me at 6:30 in the morning.
I thought someone had died.
No. Apparently my stomach was too hard.

The audience predicts one thing, you give them another.

3. Specificity Beats Generality

Funny lives in details.

Not:

My dad watches health videos.

But:

My dad watched one 30-second reel and immediately became the Chief Medical Officer of the family.

4. Escalation

Keep making the situation bigger.

Your Spanish bit does this really well. It goes:

Learn Spanish → Colombia → Cartel → Death

Each step is more ridiculous than the last.

5. The Rule of One Weird Detail

A normal story + one absurd detail.

My cousin complained to my mother about my eye contact.

Funny.

My 30-year-old cousin filed a customer support ticket with my mother about my eye contact.

Funnier.

6. Act-Outs

Don't tell me what happened.

Become the person.

You naturally do this with your dad voice.

"Your stomach should be soft."

The voice itself becomes part of the joke.

7. Contrast

Two things that shouldn't belong together.

Your material is full of this.

Ancestors survived famine and war.
I need Dave from YouTube to fall asleep.

The gap between those ideas creates comedy.

8. Callbacks

Mention something earlier, bring it back later.

Example:

Start:

My dad watched one reel.

End:

Two hours later my dad was diagnosing my liver using Instagram.

Same idea returning gets bigger laughs.

9. Say the Thing Nobody Says Out Loud

This is where your strongest material comes from.

The YouTube bit works because:

I don't watch YouTube to learn.
I watch YouTube so I don't have to hear my own thoughts.

People recognize themselves.

10. Heightening

Take a stupid premise and keep committing to it.

Your "guy living in my head" bit is basically one long heightening.

Joe Rogan knows him.
Shah Rukh is his fan.
Netflix wants him.
Madame Tussauds made a statue.

You never abandon the premise.
