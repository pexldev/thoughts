## HIS RAW DUMP (verbatim)

Why can't I quit my job. I have seen so many people being fired from their job and actually feeling better than what they were — it was as if they were in a prison and there was a case on their head and finally they are now out, and not only out, they are now proven innocent. That relief I see in people who get fired from these high-paying IT jobs — and they go on to find something for themselves: a hobby, pursue something that they purely love. They quit smoking, they sleep better, they now have more time for themselves, and they are no more thinking of killing themselves.

I can quit my job today — I have a salary worth of an entire year saved. I'm not talking about my expenditure, I'm talking about salary, and I save about 70% of it.

And I'm not happy with my job — most of the time I feel betrayed, used, taken advantage of, a victim of this entire situation. But even then I work.

I work not because I love my profession, but because society. I'm afraid of society.

If you don't work — if you just take a break — neither your company nor your client is going to catch on fire.

## THE ANGLE (parked — one line so it's not lost)

Not "corporate jobs are prisons" (LinkedIn take, everyone's heard it). The self-turn: **the cage is unlocked.** He has the money, the misery, and the evidence that leaving frees people — and still won't walk out. So the unanswerable question = "if the door's open and I stay, who's actually the guard?" The jailer is imported ("society") — same imaginary-villain move as ep18's parents/school. The confession costs HIM.
