| por meaning  | Example                                         |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------- |
| for          | gracias por todo → thanks for everything        |
| through      | camino por el parque → I walk through the park  |
| by           | hecho por Prateek → made by Prateek             |
| because of   | cancelado por lluvia → canceled because of rain |
| around/about | por aquí → around here                          |
| during       | por la noche → during the night                 |
