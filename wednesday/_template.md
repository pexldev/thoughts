## 1. COLD OPEN — the wait-WHAT line *(0:00–0:15)*

The single most extreme TRUE sentence in the story. Usually from the MIDDLE of it, not the beginning. No greeting, no context, no "so this happened."

> Corpus examples: "My dad called me at 6:30 in the morning to tell me my stomach is hard." / "My uncle got a cornea transplant. Alone." / "Fifteen years of friendship. Destroyed by a Hyundai."

**Find it:** tell the story out loud once — the sentence where a stranger would say "wait, WHAT?" is the open.
